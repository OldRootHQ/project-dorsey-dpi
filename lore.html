LORE INDEX

A searchable public glossary for OldRoot: established terminology, systems, materials, places, and concepts collected in one reference.

A / 01

Ascendant

A person transformed by the original Genesis event into a human anchored to a high-order physical domain. Ascendants are a small, finite Genesis-only category; their exact total is not established as public in-universe knowledge, and later artificial Abyron subjects are not automatically Ascendants.

A / 02

Adaptive Somatic Catalyst (ASC)

Experimental biotechnology used in Adrián Zúñiga’s bioengineering. The public origin label is Bioengineered; the technology is unrelated to Ascendant status.

Science / Biotechnology
A / 03

Abyron (Ax-126)

Element 126: a naturally occurring superheavy element discovered—not created—by Hampton Dynamics in a deep-ocean volcanic environment. Its known states include nearly transparent liquid, bright-orange ceramic, dark-orange gas, and the energized Genesis state.

Open Abyron dossier →
A / 04

Abyronic Radiation

An unusual energetic emission released aggressively by gaseous Ax-126. High-grade ceramic Abyron strongly suppresses the phenomenon and can itself serve as shielding in advanced containment systems.

Abyron engineering →
O / 05

OldRoot Power Index

A nonlinear capability framework measuring Strength, Durability, Speed, Agility, Regeneration, Senses, Offense, Intellect, Combat, Mobility, and Stamina. It does not predict the winner of a fight.

Systems / Analytics
G / 06

Genesis

The foundational Saint Dorsey catastrophe: an accidental HVA-01 containment failure in which Hampton safety systems unknowingly trigger volumetric bloom, the crystallized Ax-126 damages nearby energy infrastructure, a conventional explosion occurs, and roughly three seconds later the remaining lattice enters the brilliant Genesis state.

Open Genesis lore →
A / 07

Abyron Powder

Genesis destroys or transforms the useful intact HVA-01 sample at Saint Dorsey, leaving altered particulate material later known as Abyron Powder. It is not intact Abyron, and the unresolved material becomes part of the reason Hampton returns to the ocean for another sample.

Open Abyron Powder subdivision →
H / 08

Hampton Dynamics

One of Earth’s most advanced technology companies before Genesis. Its deep-ocean program discovered HVA-01, later identified as Abyron. Ax-126 became Hampton’s holy-grail material breakthrough rather than the source of the company’s technological strength.

Abyron dossier →
H / 09

Discovery of Abyron (HVA-01)

The first-voyage discovery record: downward-moving bubbles, a nearly invisible liquid membrane, accidental volumetric bloom during ascent, submarine failure, liquid reversion at depth, apparent quantity increase, and the sample’s return to Saint Dorsey shortly before Genesis.

Open Discovery lore →
N / 10

Non-Ascendant

A registry distinction for a person whose powers or heroic status do not come from the original Genesis transformation. OldRoot includes many unrelated origin branches.

A registry classification separate from OPI. It describes a character’s broad power class; OPI separately measures individual capability categories.

S / 12

St. Dorsey

A roughly seven-square-mile island associated with the greater Washington, D.C. area. Its Hampton deep-ocean division becomes ground zero for Genesis, then later expands into the Dorsey New General Facility as the island recovers and enters a major industrial boom.

L / 14

Los Moralistas

A fictional criminal cartel with its core in Mexico, a meaningful but limited U.S. border presence, a direct history with the ASC experiment that altered Gila Monster, and later access to diverted Ax-126 through the international supply chain.

U / 15

UMWR

Unstable Maximum Weight Response: the highest repeatable anomalous weight response an Abyron batch produces under standardized Hampton testing. Repeated volumetric bloom permanently lowers a batch’s future UMWR.

Open UMWR record →
V / 16

Volumetric Bloom

A controlled Ax-126 expansion process triggered by a specific combination of very high starting pressure, gradual decompression, rapid cooling, confinement, and trapped oxygen. Bloom increases usable volume but permanently degrades material quality.
