LORE INDEX

A searchable public glossary for OldRoot: established terminology, systems, materials, places, and concepts collected in one reference.

A / 01

Ascendant

One of the exactly 77 beings who underwent Ascension when the black Genesis wave passed through them. Ascension grants capability, not automatic mastery.

Genesis / Classification
A / 02

Adaptive Somatic Catalyst (ASC)

Experimental biotechnology used in Adrián Zúñiga’s bioengineering. The public origin label is Bioengineered; the technology is unrelated to Ascendant status.

Science / Biotechnology
D / 03

OldRoot Power Index

A nonlinear capability framework measuring Strength, Durability, Speed, Agility, Regeneration, Senses, Offense, Intellect, Combat, Mobility, and Stamina. It does not predict the winner of a fight.

Systems / Analytics
G / 04

Genesis

The foundational St. Dorsey historical event: a missile strike on a Hampton Dynamics research facility followed roughly three seconds later by a black surface-bound wave that created exactly 77 Ascendants.

Event Index →
G / 05

Genesis Dust

Black residue created as a byproduct of Genesis. It is not Hampton’s Matter. Its core distinction is amplification of properties or performance, and it cannot create a true Ascendant.

Genesis / Material
H / 06

Hampton Dynamics

One of Earth’s most technologically advanced companies. Its extreme-depth exploration program accidentally discovered Hampton’s Matter before Genesis. Its public history is not reducible to either pure villainy or pure innocence.

Organization / Science
H / 07

Hampton’s Matter

A reality-defying substance discovered—not created—during unrelated deep-ocean exploration. Repeated measurements can produce contradictory properties without the instruments necessarily being wrong.

Science / Foundational Material
N / 08

Non-Ascendant

A registry distinction for a person whose powers or heroic status do not come from becoming one of the 77 Genesis Ascendants. OldRoot includes many unrelated origin branches.

Classification
P / 09

Power Classification

A registry classification separate from OPI. It describes a character’s broad power class; OPI separately measures individual capability categories.

Systems / Registry
S / 10

St. Dorsey

A roughly seven-square-mile island district associated with the greater Washington, D.C. area. It contains residential and commercial areas, a university, and the Hampton Dynamics facility struck on Genesis night.

OldRoot Standard

The working publishing format: prose carries the complete story while selective illustrated or collage-style sequences define the moments that deserve to become iconic.

L / 12

Los Moralistas

A fictional criminal cartel with its core in Mexico, a meaningful but limited U.S. border presence, and a direct history with the ASC experiment that altered Gila Monster.
