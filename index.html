THE BEGINNING

OLDROOT TAKES ROOT.

A new creative brand begins with a simple mandate: build original worlds that can grow without becoming generic, disposable, or trapped by their own continuity.

OldRoot is the brand behind the work currently taking shape here. The first active universe is Project Dorsey, a superhero setting designed to support a large cast, distinct origins, strong visual identity, and stories that can expand for years without forcing every character through the same mythology.

This site is not the Master Bible. It is the public-facing layer around the work: character discovery, organizations, interactive analytics, and eventually the pieces of the universe that are meant to be explored rather than buried in development documents.

“Build deep roots.
Grow strange branches.”
