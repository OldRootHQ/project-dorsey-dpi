Gila Monster

Detective Adrián Zúñiga survived the ASC experiment that changed his body. His nocturnal capabilities, desert movement, regeneration, senses, and self-built equipment make him dangerous for very different reasons at once.

EXCEPTIONAL HUMAN · CHICAGO

Commotion

Ja’Kori Benson has no powers. His advantage is agility, timing, dirty fighting, awareness, and what happens when a controlled fight becomes a problem nobody else prepared for.
