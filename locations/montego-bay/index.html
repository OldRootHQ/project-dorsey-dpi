MONTEGO BAY / JAMAICA

The birthplace and hometown of Captain Marlon McKenzie, known as Liontone, a national guardian active throughout Jamaica.

WORLD INDEX IDLOC-008Established setting
OVERVIEW

Jamaica in the OldRoot Universe

Montego Bay in St. James Parish, Jamaica, is the established birthplace and hometown of Captain Marlon McKenzie / Liontone. The hero's wider military responsibilities extend across the nation; an index marker over Montego Bay identifies his hometown, not the position of his unit or every mission.

Jamaica enters the published OldRoot setting through Liontone, a decorated Jamaican Defence Force special-operations commander whose biogenetic enhancement strengthens an already exceptional soldier.

ESTABLISHED CONNECTION

Jamaica's Guardian

Unlike an independent masked vigilante, Liontone is a publicly recognized soldier and active-duty captain. His reputation rests on disciplined military leadership, protection of others, and his country's trust.

His shield and golden lion identity are recognizable across his visual design. Specific classified program locations, military installations, operational deployments and private family addresses are not published in this location record.
