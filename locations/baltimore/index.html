BALTIMORE, MARYLAND

The established home base and primary operating city of Kincast, and an established operating city of Anchorage.

WORLD INDEX IDLOC-005Established location
OVERVIEW

Location Overview

Baltimore is the established home and primary operating city of Micah and Naomi Ellison, who share the heroic identity Kincast.

It is also the established operating city of Anchorage, an active recurring underworld villain and mercenary whose personal ambition is dominance over Baltimore's criminal ecosystem rather than political control of the city.

Micah works as a barber at an unnamed neighborhood barbershop that he does not currently own. His long-term civilian ambition is to eventually own a neighborhood shop of his own.
