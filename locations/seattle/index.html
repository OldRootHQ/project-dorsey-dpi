SEATTLE / PUGET SOUND

The established primary location of Agent Emerald.

WORLD INDEX IDLOC-006Established location
OVERVIEW

Location Overview

Seattle, Washington and the surrounding Puget Sound region are the established primary location of Remington “Remy” Hampton, the vigilante known as Agent Emerald.

The current public record connects the region to Remy’s civilian work through Dunamis Dynamics and to the Hampton-era operations that push him into fully establishing the Agent Emerald identity.

HAMPTON-ERA ACTIVITY

Off-Book Annex Operation

After Mark and Remy are forced out of Hampton Dynamics, Remy discovers a larger off-book operation at a separate Seattle / Puget Sound logistics and fabrication annex. This is explicitly not the St. Dorsey facility.

Remy enters that operation with the completed Agent Emerald suit, Vector Talons, and Line Driver. A burn protocol forces him to choose between preserving evidence and rescuing workers; he chooses the workers. Public footage leaks from the incident, prompting Seattle media to temporarily call him “The Seattle Savior,” a nickname Remy hates.
