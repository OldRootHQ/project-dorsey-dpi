ST. DORSEY ISLAND

The greater Washington, D.C.-area island where an accidental Abyron containment failure became Genesis—and where Hampton’s post-disaster expansion later reshaped the local economy.

WORLD INDEX IDLOC-004Established location
OVERVIEW

Location Overview

St. Dorsey is a fictional island of roughly seven square miles associated with the greater Washington, D.C. area. Its exact public map coordinates remain schematic.

Before Genesis, the island hosts a specialized Hampton Dynamics deep-ocean division. After Genesis, Saint Dorsey remains a functioning place rather than a permanent exclusion zone. The catastrophe becomes both the island’s defining tragedy and the beginning of an enormous Hampton-driven industrial expansion.

BEFORE GENESIS

Saint Dorsey Division

The Hampton presence is not corporate headquarters. The division focuses on sonar, deep-ocean exploration, ocean mapping, marine robotics, undersea discovery, and technology for reaching parts of Earth’s oceans that conventional institutions have barely explored.

That mission is why the first HVA-01 / Abyron sample returns here after its discovery voyage. Genesis happens very early in humanity’s relationship with the element; Saint Dorsey is not hosting a giant premium-Abyron stockpile or a planned superhuman experiment.

The Saint Dorsey division remains separate from the Seattle / Puget Sound logistics and fabrication annex later connected to Agent Emerald.

The pre-Genesis Hampton workforce is approximately 400 employees.

GENESIS

An accident, a sealed mystery, and 362 deaths.

A compromised containment seal allows pressure to bleed from the newly recovered HVA-01 sample. Hampton safety systems rapidly cool and stabilize it, unknowingly triggering another volumetric bloom. The crystallizing Ax-116 changes its effective load distribution, damages nearby high-density energy infrastructure, fractures, and begins releasing dark-orange vapor.

A major conventional explosion occurs. Approximately three seconds later, the remaining Ax-116 enters catastrophic cascading failure and becomes brilliant-orange Genesis-state Abyron behind a nearly black advancing phase front.

Saint Dorsey is engulfed in a dense orange cloud for roughly 14 hours. The island remains under controlled exclusion for approximately two days. The established death toll is 362.

ASCENDANTS

The changed survivors are not identified all at once.

Routine post-Genesis medical screening does not reliably identify true Ascendants. Their manifestations can appear immediately or much later, and the world gradually realizes that some Saint Dorsey survivors were changed.

The Ascendant population is a small, finite group tied specifically to the original Genesis event. The exact total is not established as public in-universe knowledge.

RECOVERY

Saint Dorsey is not permanently quarantined.

Once the orange cloud has dissipated and the controlled exclusion period ends, search, recovery, medical screening, investigation, environmental testing, and rebuilding continue. The island is not abandoned, left apocalyptic, or converted into a permanent government exclusion zone.

Hampton faces investigation, liability, regulation, survivor and family claims, and major scrutiny while ultimately acknowledging that a newly discovered element caused the catastrophic containment failure.

DORSEY NEW GENERAL FACILITY

Hampton rebuilds much larger.

After Genesis destroys or alters the original intact Abyron sample, Hampton returns to the ocean looking for another. Later reservoir discoveries turn Ax-116 into a strategic global resource.

Hampton rebuilds and massively expands its Saint Dorsey operation into the Dorsey New General Facility: a major Abyron processing center, manufacturing complex, research site, logistics center, and strategic company facility.

The Hampton workforce grows from roughly 400 employees before Genesis to roughly 4,000 after the rebuild.

ECONOMIC BOOM

Tragedy and growth exist at the same time.

The expanded Hampton presence increases housing demand, restaurant and bar traffic, contractor work, infrastructure investment, local spending, employment, and corporate influence. Saint Dorsey remains culturally recognizable rather than becoming a default dystopian company city.

Genesis is remembered as a human catastrophe while also becoming the beginning of an enormous economic and industrial boom. That contradiction is part of the island’s long-term identity.
