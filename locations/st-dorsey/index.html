ST. DORSEY ISLAND

The quarantined Washington, D.C.-area island where Genesis created exactly seventy-seven Ascendants.

WORLD INDEX IDLOC-004Established location
OVERVIEW

Location Overview

St. Dorsey is a Washington, D.C.-area island of roughly seven square miles with an established resident population of approximately 2,350. About 4,500 people were physically present when Genesis occurred.

The island became the site of the defining Genesis event after a missile struck a Hampton facility used for specialized sonar and submarine systems storage and development. The conventional blast was followed by a black, surface-bound shockwave associated with Hampton’s Matter. Exactly seventy-seven people underwent instantaneous Ascension.

HAMPTON FACILITY

Established Function

The Hampton facility on St. Dorsey is specifically established as a site for specialized sonar and submarine systems storage and development. It is not the separate Seattle / Puget Sound logistics and fabrication annex later encountered by Agent Emerald.

KNOWN ACTIVITY

Known Activity

Genesis lasted only seconds but transformed the island’s place in the world. Approximately 500 people died and roughly 4,000 survived among those physically present. The island was subsequently quarantined.

The Genesis wave did not cover the entire island indiscriminately and did not affect aircraft above it. Exact public map coordinates for St. Dorsey are not published, so its globe marker is approximate.
