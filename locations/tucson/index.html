TUCSON, ARIZONA

The established operating city of Gila Monster and a major point in his investigation into Los Moralistas.

WORLD INDEX IDLOC-001Established location
OVERVIEW

Location Overview

Tucson is the primary established operating location of Adrián Zúñiga, the Tucson Police Department detective who becomes Gila Monster after surviving an ASC experiment.

The city is part of a broader border-region criminal landscape tied to Los Moralistas, but cartel presence is not the same as territorial control. The organization’s U.S. footprint is deliberately limited and dispersed rather than presented as owning the city.

KNOWN ACTIVITY

Known Activity

Gila Monster’s detective work, nocturnal operations, surveillance, tracking, and desert-specific movement are rooted here. Specific public event chronology remains open for future stories.
