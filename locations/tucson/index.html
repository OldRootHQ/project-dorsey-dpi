TUCSON, ARIZONA

The established operating city of Gila Monster and a major point in his investigation into Los Moralistas.

WORLD INDEX IDLOC-001Established location
OVERVIEW

Location Overview

Tucson is the primary established operating location of Adrián Zúñiga, the Tucson Police Department detective who becomes Gila Monster after surviving a bioengineering experiment involving the Adaptive Somatic Catalyst.

The city is part of a broader border-region criminal landscape tied to Los Moralistas, but cartel presence is not the same as territorial control. The organization’s U.S. footprint is deliberately limited and dispersed rather than presented as owning the city.
