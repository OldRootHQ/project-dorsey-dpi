ABOUT OLDROOT

OldRoot is the brand behind the creative work being developed here.

OldRoot is being built for long-form original worlds. The goal is not to manufacture a pile of interchangeable superheroes. It is to create characters, places, organizations, visual identities, and stories with enough structure to matter and enough open space to keep growing.

Project Dorsey is the universe currently in active development. Its internal Master Bible remains the continuity source of truth. This website is a separate presentation layer: a place to discover characters, explore organizations, and use tools such as DPI Analytics without turning the development Bible itself into a public database.

As OldRoot grows, this page can grow with it. For now, it says only what has actually been established.
