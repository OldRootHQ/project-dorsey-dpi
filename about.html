THE STUDIO

Not a content factory.

OldRoot exists to build worlds with enough structure to matter and enough freedom to keep growing.

The aim is not a pile of interchangeable superheroes. Characters, places, organizations, visual identities, technologies, histories, and future mythologies should feel like parts of a world rather than inventory.

Project Dorsey is the first active OldRoot universe. The internal Master Bible remains the private continuity source of truth; this website is the public presentation layer built for readers and discovery.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Familiar powers can still produce unfamiliar heroes.

Originality comes from mythology, character, rules, aesthetics, cultures, consequences, and execution—not from banning recognizable superpowers.

Strong silhouettes

Characters should be readable before a paragraph of explanation is required.

Clear mechanics

Rules give powers scene language and make spectacle more memorable rather than less imaginative.

Room to expand

Origins, cosmology, technology, magic, genetics, aliens, artifacts, martial arts, experiments, and future branches can coexist without one explanation swallowing the universe.
