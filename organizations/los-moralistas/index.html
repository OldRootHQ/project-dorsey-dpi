LOS MORALISTAS

A transnational fictional cartel tied directly to Gila Monster’s origin and the Adaptive Somatic Catalyst.

REGISTRY STATUSACTIVEPublic profile established
OVERVIEW

Organization Overview

Los Moralistas is a fictional criminal cartel whose core power base is in Mexico. Its U.S. presence is meaningful but deliberately sparse: border-region operations and scattered interior logistics rather than total territorial control.

The organization is part of Gila Monster’s history. While investigating Los Moralistas, Tucson detective Adrián Zúñiga was captured and subjected to an experiment involving the Adaptive Somatic Catalyst, or ASC. He survived the procedure and later became Gila Monster.

ASC CONNECTION

Adaptive Somatic Catalyst

ASC is the experimental biotechnology used on Adrián. Los Moralistas functioned as customer, funder, and operator in the experiment. The organization is not currently established as the inventor of ASC, and the technology’s upstream supplier remains open.

OPEN DEVELOPMENT

Unresolved Public Details

Leadership, supplier relationships, surviving experimental subjects, named corrupt officials, and the cartel’s full internal hierarchy remain intentionally unestablished. The public site will not invent those details ahead of the stories.
