DUNAMIS DYNAMICS

Mark Hampton’s company and the established civilian technology base connected to Agent Emerald.

REGISTRY STATUSESTABLISHEDPublic profile established
OVERVIEW

Organization Overview

Dunamis Dynamics is the smaller company founded by Mark Hampton after leaving Hampton Dynamics.

Its established areas are sanitation systems, weapons technology, weapon attachments and accessories, and advanced prototypes. Remy uses Dunamis facilities and maintains private development sectors there.

LEADERSHIP & ROLE

Known Leadership

Mark Hampton is the founder of Dunamis Dynamics. Remington “Remy” Hampton serves as Executive Vice President of Advanced Systems & Prototyping.

OWNERSHIP BOUNDARY

VX-11

VX-11 belongs to Remy personally. It is his design and intellectual property and is not automatically property of Dunamis Dynamics.

Its construction uses plausible advanced real-world materials and process architecture rather than magical fictional unobtainium.

BIOLOGICAL ENHANCEMENT

Precision / Reflex Stimulant

The Precision / Reflex Stimulant was developed by Dunamis Dynamics under Mark Hampton’s biological-enhancement oversight. It temporarily improves neural processing, reaction initiation, peripheral threat registration, and sensory-response efficiency for approximately a couple of hours.

The stimulant does not grant precognition, bullet time, automatic evasion, or magically faster muscles.
