OR-WEB-0045 · October 6, 2026 · Genesis Canon Rewrite

Genesis is rebuilt around the Saint Dorsey accident.

The Genesis record now follows the authoritative accidental-failure chain: the first HVA-01 recovery, a compromised Saint Dorsey containment seal, safety systems unknowingly triggering another bloom, UMWR load shift, high-density energy-system damage, the first explosion, and the iconic three-second delay before Genesis-state Abyron engulfs the island.

The rewrite establishes the 14-hour orange cloud, roughly two-day exclusion period, 362 deaths, delayed and medically undetectable Ascendant emergence, a small finite Genesis-only Ascendant category, Abyron Powder, Hampton’s transparent-but-liable response, the post-Genesis reservoir search, and the Dorsey New General Facility. Earlier 77-Ascendant, giant-stockpile, Genesis-Dust, permanent-quarantine, and unknown-attacker framing is superseded.
