FEATURED / SITE UPDATE
September 2026 · OldRoot Studios

The site is becoming the universe’s front door.

OldRoot’s first public site began as a functional registry. The current rebuild pushes it toward something more deliberate: cleaner typography, stronger hierarchy, deeper world navigation, an actual lore index, an events registry, a guided Start Here page, and a modern dispatch desk.

The next visual phase will replace temporary and overly photoreal character imagery with a more intentional comic-editorial art direction.
