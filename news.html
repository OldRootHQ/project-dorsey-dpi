FEATURED / SITE UPDATE
September 2026 · OldRoot Studios

The site is becoming the universe’s front door.

OldRoot’s first public site began as a functional registry. The current rebuild pushes it toward something more deliberate: cleaner typography, stronger hierarchy, deeper world navigation, an actual lore index, an events registry, a guided Start Here page, and a modern dispatch desk.

The next visual phase will replace temporary and overly photoreal character imagery with a more intentional comic-editorial art direction.

SITE PATCH LOG

Public change record.

Meaningful website repairs and system changes are logged here as they ship.

OR-WEB-0025 · October 3, 2026 · Visual System

The Living Root Field deepens the After Dark identity.

OldRoot now carries a continuous organic root layer beneath its editorial surfaces, with darker environmental depth, sparse network nodes, restrained ambient motion, and a more open World Index rhythm. The effect is shared across public After Dark pages, respects reduced-motion settings, and uses a lightweight SVG/CSS treatment instead of heavy background imagery.

View the updated homepage →
OR-WEB-0024 · October 3, 2026 · Development Registry

Kalani and Duke enter the upcoming-character seed list.

Both names now appear anywhere the public site previews upcoming OldRoot characters, keeping the homepage and Dispatch Desk development lists synchronized without publishing unrevealed character details.

View Upcoming Characters →
OR-WEB-0023 · October 1, 2026 · Character Artwork

Agent Emerald and Aftermark receive their creator-art packages.

Agent Emerald now carries distinct Seattle combat, mobility, registry, dossier, and featured visuals, while Aftermark's former design-sheet filler has been fully replaced across the site. Aftermark's horizontal pieces are reserved for cover/card roles, with vertical art used inside his dossier.

Open Agent Emerald → Open Aftermark →
OR-WEB-0021 · October 1, 2026 · Character Registry

Agent Emerald enters the public OldRoot registry.

Remington “Remy” Hampton now has a canon-grounded dossier, exact OPI profile, Seattle / Puget Sound location record, and Dunamis Dynamics organization record. His official emblem and character artwork remain intentionally unassigned until creator-supplied designs are available.

Open Agent Emerald →
OR-WEB-0016 · October 1, 2026 · Sitewide Redesign

OldRoot After Dark now spans the full public site.

The dark green visual system has expanded from the homepage into characters, dossiers, Lore, Events, Organizations, Locations, OPI, Library, commerce, Dispatches, Contact, Support, and every shared site surface.

OR-WEB-0015 · October 1, 2026 · Visual Prototype

OldRoot After Dark begins on the homepage.

The homepage is testing a darker cinematic direction built from near-black green, root-network atmosphere, restrained gold signal marks, larger character artwork, and layered translucent surfaces. The rest of the site remains unchanged while the direction is reviewed.

View Homepage →
OR-WEB-0014 · October 1, 2026 · Navigation

OldRoot's masthead now uses grouped navigation.

Characters now exposes the Character Registry and OPI Analytics, World groups Locations, Events, and Organizations, and Lore has its own menu for Start Here and the Lore Index. Desktop hover, click, keyboard, and mobile tap interactions are supported.

OR-WEB-0013 · October 1, 2026 · Locations Globe

The OldRoot globe now supports mobile pinch zoom.

Touch devices can now pinch the globe itself to zoom, horizontally drag to rotate, and still use a normal vertical swipe to scroll the page. The existing globe buttons remain available.
