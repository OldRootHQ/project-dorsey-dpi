# OldRoot Visual Identity — Production Website Standard (HQ 2.4)

This is the current visual reference for the public OldRoot Studios website. It supersedes the early café/parchment website palette and historical HQ 2.1–2.2 draft accents. Preserve creator-approved artwork and character-specific visual identities.

## Current website palette

- Cosmic background — deep emerald-black (`#030B09`) with the approved green nebula asset.
- Matte-carbon surfaces — smooth dark emerald composite (`--or-composite` in `brand.css`); **no flannel, crosshatch, checkerboard or rustic paper texture**.
- Soft avocado accent — `#B7CF8A` (`--or-avocado` / `--or-lime`).
- Pale avocado — `#D5E5B7` (`--or-avocado-light`).
- Comic white — `#FAFFFC` (`--or-comic-white`) for primary headings and text anchors.
- Cool muted copy — `#C9D9CF` (`--or-ink-muted`) or a higher-contrast light variant on dark cards.
- Dark ink for light avocado panels — deep forest green (`#173621`).

## Production usage rules

- Use the emerald-black cosmic field behind the page, dark matte-carbon cards for most editorial and technical surfaces, comic-white headings, and readable cool-light body copy.
- Use soft avocado for selected labels, active controls, borders and emphasis. A few deliberately light avocado feature panels are welcome **only with dark green text**; avoid making every card bright.
- Keep interface strokes green, not inherited café brown or repeated gold. Do not bring back sepia, parchment, faux-vintage paper or flannel-like crosshatching.
- Keep the approved logos, emblems, cover art and portraits unmodified. The world globe's internal gold markers and character-specific gold details are intentional exceptions, not a license to recolor the site UI.
- Keep typography readable on desktop and phones, and preserve the working navigation, globe, OPI readouts, lightboxes, forms, polls and Marketplace status.
- Never add graffiti, text or root overlays to the sitewide cosmic background.
- Use scoped page styles when a page needs a different visual hierarchy; do not reintroduce old global overrides to solve a local problem.
- Visual maintenance belongs in `PATCHLOG.md`, not a new public Dispatch.

## Historical palette (archive only, not the default website UI)

They should not override the approved current website presentation.

## Public character dossier editorial standard

- Treat every published character as a confident in-universe record, not a list of pending writer decisions.
- If a school, employer, mission, family detail, emblem, date, or other optional detail is unwritten, omit it. Never surface phrases such as "not yet established," "intentionally unresolved," "design not established," "TBD," or "open writer decisions" in public character copy.
- Preserve mysteries and story flexibility without inventing answers: a covert organization can remain unnamed, a ritual's history can be mysterious, and relationships can evolve without public author notes explaining what has not been plotted.
- Use a neutral OldRoot Archive dossier mark where a character does not use a published insignia. Do not imply a missing logo is a production error.
- Keep individual OPI/DPI measurements distinct from official aggregate ratings. Describe analytics-only means as references, never as missing character information.
- Reserve production planning and canon guardrails for internal notes and tests; public paragraphs should convey characters, world facts, and story stakes.

## Site principle

Different page families may vary the balance of matte carbon, emerald depth and occasional avocado feature panels, but they must retain one clear OldRoot identity. Specialized systems such as the interactive globe and OPI charts keep their approved functional color coding and interactions.
