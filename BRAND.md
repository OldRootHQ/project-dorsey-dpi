# OldRoot Visual Identity — Working Brand Guide v0.1

This is a website/brand design reference. It is not Project Dorsey story canon.

## Core palette

- Root Green — `#244C3A`
- Deep Root Green — `#173428`
- Cream — `#F3EBDD`
- Soft Parchment — `#E7DCCB`
- Café Brown — `#8C6A4A`
- Dark Bark — `#5F4633`
- Silver — `#B7BEC5`
- Slate Silver — `#8E979F`
- Old Gold — `#C5A24A`
- Ink Dark — `#151515`

## Usage

- Forest greens are the primary brand identity and structural colors.
- Cream and parchment carry editorial surfaces, reading areas, and warm contrast.
- Café brown and bark provide earth-toned secondary accents.
- Silver is a functional neutral for borders, metadata, and technical UI.
- Old Gold is a controlled emphasis color for active navigation, highlights, data points, and premium accents.
- Avoid bright lemon yellow, neon green, rainbow UI, faux-rustic textures, and excessive vintage/sepia treatment.
- The intended tone is modern, premium, organic, archival, and deliberate — not coffee-shop branding.

## Site principle

Different sections may shift light/dark balance while staying inside the same palette. Editorial OldRoot pages may favor cream; technical surfaces such as DPI Analytics may favor Deep Root Green. Shared navigation, accent rules, and palette maintain one brand identity.
