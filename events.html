ST. DORSEY / GENESIS NIGHT

GENESIS

A catastrophic impact struck the specialized Hampton Dynamics site on St. Dorsey. The initial destruction came first. Roughly three seconds later, hundreds of kilograms of unusually high-grade ceramic Ax-126 reached cascading lattice failure and entered the brilliant energized state now associated with Genesis.

The iconic black wave is the advancing phase boundary of the event, with dense bright-orange Genesis-state Abyron moving behind and within it along the island surface. Exactly 77 humans successfully stabilized through the transformation and became Ascendants. The catastrophe also produced Genesis Dust, caused widespread physical and electronic disruption, and pushed St. Dorsey into quarantine and controlled-access status. The exact villain, weapon, and final attack mechanism remain unrevealed.
