ST. DORSEY / GENESIS NIGHT

GENESIS

An advanced missile directly struck the reinforced Hampton Dynamics warehouse/laboratory on St. Dorsey. A conventional explosion came first. Roughly three seconds later, a black surface-bound wave erupted outward.

Exactly 77 beings Ascended at the instant that wave passed through them. The event also left behind black Genesis Dust, caused widespread physical and electronic disruption, and pushed St. Dorsey into quarantine and controlled-access status.

EVENT STANDARD

What earns a place here?

The event registry is intentionally harder to enter than a character biography.

Wider consequences

An event belongs here when it materially affects broader public history, geography, culture, technology, superhuman society, international relations, or multiple otherwise-unconnected groups.

Character events stay personal

A hero’s defining fight can be enormous to that character without automatically becoming a world-history entry.

No duplicate chronology

This page documents historical significance. It is not a second timeline and does not copy every personal detail from individual dossiers.
