SAKURO

Kenji Kuroda

Compassion is his first instinct. The blossoms are what happen when compassion has to fight back.

Sakura InsigniaSAKURO

Overview

Kenji Kuroda, known as Sakuro, is a 27-year-old Japanese immigrant and florist living in San Diego, California. Together with his wife Hannah, he has built a peaceful civilian life around their flower shop. His extraordinary ability to generate and control cherry-blossom petals came from an elderly stranger whose final message was omoiyari — thoughtful compassion toward others.

Sakuro is a highly capable supernatural protector with powers spanning defense, containment, and formidable offensive techniques. He prefers protecting people and restraining opponents, but his kindness should never be mistaken for an inability to fight.

Biography

A life in San Diego

Kenji moved from Japan to San Diego at age 20. He is fluent in English and works as a florist in the shop founded by Hannah. They met when she was working as a grocery-store cashier; Kenji became a familiar customer and eventually asked her out. The two married before Kenji inherited his powers.

The four flowers

One day, an elderly homeless Japanese veteran visited the shop and purchased a single flower with his own money. Kenji gave him three additional flowers. The man explained that he liked using what little money he had to make the city more beautiful.

Unseen by Kenji, the veteran planted the three gifted flowers outside the shop and the purchased flower near his camp. Their friendship developed through occasional visits, food, and ordinary acts of consideration. The veteran was not Kenji's martial-arts instructor or formal mentor.

Omoiyari

When the elderly man eventually died naturally of old age, Kenji was present. Before his passing, the veteran transferred his extraordinary cherry-blossom powers to Kenji. His final word was omoiyari. The origin of the veteran's power and the precise method of its transfer remain unknown.

Personality

Kenji is cheerful, considerate, gentle, and naturally approachable. His broad, sometimes eyes-closed smile and bashful habit of scratching the back of his head contrast with the commanding appearance of his armored identity. He can be overly trusting and sometimes struggles to stand up for himself.

He endured cruelty during childhood, but that history does not define him as a product of suffering. His compassion is part of who he chooses to be. Under serious pressure, Kenji can become deliberate, cold, and frighteningly decisive without gaining power from anger.

Powers & Abilities

Blossom creation and control

Sakuro generates physical cherry-blossom petals near his body and actively manipulates them within a maximum radius of 288 feet. He can create and direct thousands of petals, with no established fixed count limit. Creating fresh petals costs more stamina than moving existing ones. Petals outside his control radius fall or drift naturally; he cannot conjure them directly around a distant opponent.

Defense and protection

Rotating petal formations can capture incoming bullets, form multidirectional shields, and defend several people at once. He can surround an explosive before detonation or use barriers to reduce blast and shrapnel exposure. Barriers can be overwhelmed or rupture under excessive force, and escaped explosions can still injure Kenji. His protection is consciously directed, not an automatic danger sense.

Restraint and battlefield control

Sakuro binds limbs, contains opponents inside swirling formations, applies focused restraint pressure, and interferes with movement. These techniques support his preference for nonlethal resolution and give him powerful control of crowded or complex engagements.

Cutting and explosive techniques

During an active slicing technique, selected petals become dense, razor-sharp, and faintly pink-luminous. He can shape them into cutting discs or steer single large and multiple smaller blossom tornadoes. Petal bursts can stun and disorient, while Explosive Scatter distributes petals before triggering a stronger series of attacks.

His blossom tornadoes are dangerous primarily because of their cutting petals, not supernatural wind. Cutting petals return to their soft state when the technique stops or when they strike material they cannot penetrate. Scattered petals may be regrouped after a brief interval and repurposed for restraints.

Limits

Kenji remains physiologically human. He has no established accelerated healing, superhuman muscular enhancement, flight, or precognitive senses. Generating large quantities of petals consumes stamina; complex manipulation requires concentration; the 288-foot active radius cannot be exceeded. If he loses consciousness, every active petal formation loses control. Wind can complicate manipulation but does not universally disable his powers.

Combat Style

At peak capability, Sakuro integrates defensive layers, ranged cutting formations, multi-target restraints, evasive movement, and trained swordsmanship. His ability to switch between protection and offense makes him an exceptionally dangerous battlefield controller. Anger does not multiply his power; when pressed, he may deliberately stop holding back.

His ordinary steel katana helps guide petal trajectories and remains a practical weapon. He had little or no prior formal sword training when he inherited the abilities and developed his technique afterward.

Equipment & Appearance

Sakuro wears a fitted pearl-white combat one-piece with low-profile protective armor, structural charcoal-black panels, bold sakura-pink shoulders, and a prominent pink cherry-blossom chest insignia. His gloves and boots are completely black. A white half-cowl mask carries pink details and a small sakura emblem while leaving his lower face visible.

His black hair remains exposed above the cowl, and his natural dark-brown eyes shift to sakura pink only during active power use. He carries a conventional steel katana with a pink-and-white wrapped grip and traditional guard.

The current illustrations are creator-approved temporary character references, intended to be succeeded by commissioned artwork.

Relationships

Hannah

Kenji's wife, Hannah, is an artistic, warm, and pragmatic florist who founded the flower shop they operate. Her love of floral arrangements began with her grandmother, whose natural, asymmetrical arranging technique Hannah still practices. Their marriage predates Sakuro's powers and anchors his civilian life.

The elderly veteran

A homeless Japanese elder who was often dismissed or mistreated by others. Kenji's ordinary compassion toward him led to a life-changing friendship. His identity and military history have not yet been established.
