LIONTONE

Captain Marlon McKenzie

An enhanced Jamaican special-operations commander whose imposing presence and disciplined judgment have made him a national guardian.

Jamaica’s guardianLIONTONE

Jamaica's National Guardian

Captain Marlon McKenzie, known publicly as Liontone, is a decorated Jamaican Defence Force special-operations commander and one of his nation's formidable protectors. Born and raised in Montego Bay, St. James Parish, Marlon was already an exceptional soldier before undergoing a classified biogenetic enhancement procedure.

Standing at six-foot-four, he cuts an intimidating figure in emerald-green, black and muted gold. Behind that presence is a respectful, compassionate, principled gentleman whose greatest strength is as much his judgment as his physical power.

He carries a golden lion insignia on his uniform, a circular ballistic shield and a combat knife. His public identity is known; his military service remains active.

Origin & Biogenetic Enhancement

Prior to enhancement, Marlon had earned distinction through special-operations training, leadership and field experience. A classified biogenetic procedure subsequently elevated his strength, durability, speed, agility, stamina, sensory awareness and natural recovery.

The enhancement is balanced, not boundless. Marlon is neither invulnerable nor among the universe's highest-powered superhumans. His elite training, practical intelligence and military discipline remain essential to everything he does. The program's undisclosed participants and technical details are not public.

Military Service & Combat

Marlon commands a specialized six-to-eight-person Jamaica Defence Force unit, the majority of its personnel unenhanced. He coordinates operations, protects his squad and adjusts to changing conditions through tactical planning rather than brute strength alone.

His specialties include close-quarters combat, firearms proficiency, shield-based defense, military leadership and adaptive battlefield engagement. The steel-and-green circular shield anchors his defensive approach, while his combat knife provides a dependable short-range option.

The Disciplined Gentleman

Underneath his stern battlefield demeanor, Marlon is considerate, loyal, articulate and quietly funny. He believes that leadership is earned by responsible decisions and the example a commander sets. He values the people under his command as individuals, not as expendable pieces of a mission.

His flaws are personal: self-criticism, emotional reserve and an instinct to shoulder too much responsibility. His drive to save everyone can become a burden for himself and those who rely on his direction. Off duty, he enjoys reading, dominoes, cards and the quieter company of people he trusts.

Family & Known Connections

Marlon is widowed and has a fourteen-year-old stepson, Kymani Reid. He maintains a guarded private life despite the public attention that comes with his role as Liontone.

Lloyd Hylton · Second-in-command / mentorJavvoy Ghost · Enhanced combat medicKymani Reid · Stepson

Lloyd Hylton is Marlon's trusted second-in-command, former instructor and mentor. Javvoy Ghost is a combat medic, protégé and trusted member of the squad. These connections are part of Liontone's established supporting cast; the identities of his remaining team members remain outside the current public record.

Appearance & Visual Identity

Marlon has rich dark-brown skin, a broad muscular build, an extended connected goatee and short, thick, irregular freeform locs that hang forward. The locs are predominantly black, with small red and green sections.

His battle uniform combines Jamaican emerald, matte black and muted gold, with a conspicuous golden lion on the chest. His circular shield has a green center and metallic outer rings. The lion displayed on his uniform is an established visual motif; no separate standalone character-emblem graphic has been supplied for the site's official emblem system.
