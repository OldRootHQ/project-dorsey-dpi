LIONTONE

Captain Marlon McKenzie

An enhanced Jamaican special-operations commander whose imposing presence and disciplined judgment have made him a national guardian.

Jamaica’s guardianLIONTONE

Overview

Captain Marlon McKenzie is a 38-year-old Jamaican Defence Force special-operations officer and a protector of Jamaica. Raised in Montego Bay, he became a respected commander through discipline, sound judgment, and formidable training long before any enhancement.

Marlon is a hero first: a calm, respectful gentleman who can be warm and funny with those closest to him, even though his intimidating appearance can suggest otherwise.

His visual identity pairs a fitted emerald-green and black superhero uniform with a gold lion insignia, a circular steel-and-emerald shield, and understated military utility.

Origin & Enhancement

Already an elite soldier, Marlon voluntarily participated in a classified enhancement initiative supported by a legitimate Jamaican defense partnership. The successful procedure modestly improved his physical capabilities across multiple dimensions without making him invulnerable.

He remains susceptible to firearms, blades, significant injury, and exhaustion. His abilities are stable but monitored, and his training and leadership remain as important as his enhanced physiology.

Service & Combat

Marlon commands a specialized six-to-eight-person military team. His approach combines close-quarters skill, firearms proficiency, shield defense, adaptive tactics, and careful battlefield leadership. He prefers to avoid lethal outcomes when feasible but accepts that protecting lives can require decisive force.

His signature equipment includes a circular shield with a broad emerald center, steel rings and restrained black-and-yellow bands, alongside a practical oversized black-and-steel military combat knife.

Personality & Relationships

Disciplined and courteous, Marlon is also self-critical and emotionally guarded. He often retreats into work when under strain. A trusted second-in-command, Lloyd Hylton, knows when to challenge him; combat medic Javvoy Ghost brings a different, more playful energy to the squad.

His determination to save others can become a leadership blind spot: the risks he chooses to take can fall on the people who follow him. Away from duty, he enjoys reading, dominoes, cards, and peaceful time at home.

Visual Identity

A muscular 6'4" Jamaican man with dark-brown skin, a connected goatee, and short, chunky freeform locs with restrained red and green dyed strands. His fitted emerald-and-black superhero costume retains a subtle special-operations influence, with the golden lion as the primary emblem. The shield repeats no lion imagery.

The costume and shield shown in approved studio artwork are the current visual references. Additional scene illustrations are being prepared separately.
