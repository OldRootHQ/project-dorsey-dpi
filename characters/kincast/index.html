KINCAST

Micah Ellison & Naomi Ellison

One heroic identity shared by a human gunfighter and his independently conscious, two-dimensional shadow twin.

Character Mark[ CHARACTER LOGO — COMING ]

Overview

Kincast is the shared heroic identity of twin siblings Micah and Naomi Ellison. Micah is a biologically human Baltimore barber and elite gunfighter. Naomi is an independently conscious two-dimensional shadow presence linked to him.

Neither is the other’s sidekick. Kincast functions as a paired combat identity: Micah provides movement, firearms, decision-making, and direct human interaction while Naomi independently watches, protects, restrains, attacks, retrieves, and reshapes herself around the same battlefield.

The exact metaphysical explanation for Naomi’s existence has not been publicly established.

Personality & Dynamic

Micah is relaxed, socially gifted, naturally funny, and good at making people comfortable. He is a smartass without becoming a constant quip machine, reads rooms well, and is usually the de-escalator between the twins.

Naomi is affectionate, playful, curious, teasing, and fiercely protective. She is also substantially more willing than Micah to escalate a dangerous situation. Micah does not control her; they ask, argue, negotiate, coordinate, and occasionally irritate each other like siblings who have spent their entire lives together.

Only Micah directly understands Naomi’s private communication. Naomi understands other people, but her responses are not naturally readable to them.

Powers & Abilities

Two-dimensional shadow existence

Naomi remains two-dimensional at all times. She can bend, fold, stretch, twist, sharpen, branch, compress, whip, rotate, flatten against surfaces, and extend through open air while retaining essentially no conventional depth.

She controls her own form rather than controlling all shadows or all darkness. Her body is visually absolute black, while her facial expressions appear as hollow negative-space cutouts rather than glowing eyes or teeth.

Physical interaction and offensive geometry

Naomi can physically interact with the environment through grabbing, restraining, pulling, disarming, striking, blocking, retrieving, and other direct contact. She can reshape herself into narrow points, broad hands, branching limbs, long cords, sheet-like restraints, and razor-like configurations.

Her threat comes from abnormal geometry and attack angles as much as raw force.

Defensive interception

Naomi can independently monitor Micah’s blind angles, intercept incoming threats, block gunfire, attack a threat he is not facing, pull him out of danger, and defend civilians while Micah remains offensively engaged.

Darkness concealment

True darkness does not depower Naomi. Instead, she becomes difficult to distinguish from the surrounding darkness and can conceal Micah within it. Kincast can move extremely quickly while operating in this concealed state, but the effect is not teleportation, intangibility, or unrestricted flight.

Dual-consciousness awareness

Kincast effectively operates with two independently observing minds. Naomi can track threats Micah is not facing and communicate danger through their private sibling language, producing unusually strong battlefield awareness without granting Micah unrelated super-senses.

Combat

Micah is an elite human combatant specializing in firearms, ambidextrous shooting, dual-wielding, movement, improvised close-quarters fighting, and the bespoke paired combat language he has developed with Naomi.

Kincast fights as two independent combatants occupying one shared identity. Micah can shoot, move, reload, and pressure enemies while Naomi simultaneously defends, restrains, attacks, manipulates position, retrieves equipment, watches blind spots, and protects civilians.

In darkness, the pair becomes especially difficult to read: opponents may see only muzzle flashes, brief movement, and sudden shadow interaction before Micah and Naomi reappear from a different angle.

Equipment

Ivory hand cannon

Micah’s primary signature handgun is a large semi-automatic ballistic sidearm with a warm ivory body, black mechanical components, and restrained burnt-amber or bronze detailing. It is deliberately heavy, controlled, and precision-oriented.

Black machine pistol

The second signature weapon is a compact near-black ballistic machine pistol with burnt-amber accents and an extended magazine. It is built around a much faster cadence and aggressive close-to-midrange pressure.

The weapons share a design language without being identical twins. Ivory represents Micah, black represents Naomi, and amber visually unifies Kincast. No official weapon names, calibers, manufacturers, or magical ammunition have been established.

Visual Identity

Micah wears warm ivory / bone superhero streetwear with burnt-orange or deep-amber accents, a fitted dark underlayer, restrained tactical details, streamlined cargo elements, and modern boots. His face remains exposed. He has no cape or hood.

Naomi supplies the dominant black mass in the composition. She should remain a flat, lightless silhouette rather than a volumetric symbiote, smoke creature, sludge effect, or generic demon. The visual rule is simple: the man gets the light; the guardian gets the darkness.
