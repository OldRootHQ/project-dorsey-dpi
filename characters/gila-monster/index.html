Overview

Adrián Zúñiga is a Tucson Police Department detective who operates after sunset as Gila Monster. Altered through the Adaptive Somatic Catalyst during his investigation of Los Moralistas, Adrián survived a process intended to create desert-adapted smugglers and soldiers and emerged as a low-level metahuman built around durability, regeneration, sensory enhancement, subterranean movement, and ambush combat.

His investigative intelligence is specialized rather than universal: detective work, surveillance, tracking, infiltration, case-solving, and planning are where he excels.

Biography

Adrián grew up without a stable family after losing his mother before he could remember her and being left by his father. He moved through foster care and orphanages before building a deliberately self-contained adult life.

He joined TPD at 22 and became the department’s youngest detective at 25. While investigating Los Moralistas, he was captured and subjected to an ASC experiment. After escaping, he returned to TPD under a cover story that he had been abducted, tested, tortured, and escaped after the cartel planted evidence suggesting his death.

He keeps the resulting abilities secret. His normal shift is 7 a.m.–3 p.m.; after sunset, he becomes Gila Monster.

Personality

Adrián is quietly charismatic, kind but cold, compassionate without being emotionally open, and comfortable living as a lone wolf. He tends to control rooms conversationally without needing to dominate them loudly. When working alone he may talk himself through strategy and evidence.

Powers & Abilities

  • Extreme-human to low-metahuman strength.
  • Thickened skin and an extraordinarily resilient skeletal structure.
  • Regeneration sufficient for small lacerations in roughly an hour and major open wounds over roughly two days; no established limb regrowth.
  • Strong metahuman agility with exceptional human speed.
  • Enhanced senses, stronger at night.
  • Physical burrowing through sand and earth, slower through rocky ground, highly restricted by concrete and bedrock, and stopped by steel barriers.
  • Underground vibration sensing.

Combat & Equipment

Gila Monster prefers ambush, rapid engagement, disengagement, repositioning, and terrain control over prolonged exchanges. His suit is flexible protective gear rather than heavy armor.

Artificial short black fingernails are integrated into his gloves. They can deliver paralytic venom, fire as projectiles, and auto-replenish, but they have clear penetration limits and are not biological Wolverine-style claws or heavy-armor cutters.
