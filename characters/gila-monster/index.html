GILA MONSTER

Adrián Zúñiga

Los Moralistas created the altered body. Gila Monster created the weapons.

Character Mark

Overview

Adrián Zúñiga is a Tucson Police Department detective who operates after dark as Gila Monster. He is a Non-Ascendant metahuman altered through the Adaptive Somatic Catalyst, or ASC, after being captured while investigating Los Moralistas.

His capabilities stay in a dangerous but grounded metahuman range: enhanced physical ability, extraordinary durability, accelerated regeneration, heightened senses, and terrain-dependent subterranean movement. His suit adds protective gear and engineered venom-delivery weapons without turning him into a heavy-armor hero.

Biography

Adrián grew up without a stable family. His mother died before he could remember her, his father was absent, and his childhood passed through foster care and orphanages. That history helped produce a self-contained adult who is comfortable living and working alone.

He joined the Tucson Police Department at twenty-two and became its youngest detective at twenty-five. By thirty-three he had built a reputation as an exceptional case-solver, particularly through patient surveillance, tracking, infiltration, planning, and an ability to control a room conversationally without needing to dominate it physically.

Los Moralistas and ASC

While investigating Los Moralistas, Adrián was captured and subjected to an experiment involving the Adaptive Somatic Catalyst. ASC radically adapts a body under trauma and environmental stress. Adrián survived and stabilized; he is not continuously evolving and cannot simply develop new powers whenever a story demands it.

After his escape, Adrián returned to police work behind a cover story built around abduction, testing, torture, escape, and a falsified death trail. He keeps his altered condition secret and divides his life between detective work and nocturnal activity as Gila Monster.

Personality & Methods

Adrián is quietly charismatic, compassionate, cold when necessary, and highly self-contained. He can be kind without becoming outwardly warm, and he often talks through problems to himself as part of his strategic process.

His intelligence is specialized rather than universal. He is an excellent investigator and planner, but he is not a catch-all scientific or technological genius. He can use arrest and due process when appropriate, while his exact threshold for lethal force remains intentionally narrow and contextual rather than indiscriminate.

Powers & Abilities

Altered physiology

ASC left Adrián with thickened skin, an unusually resilient skeletal structure, enhanced strength and agility, accelerated healing, and heightened sensory capability. Small injuries can recover unusually quickly while severe wounds still require meaningful time. He does not regenerate lost limbs.

Burrowing and terrain

Gila Monster physically displaces material rather than phasing through it. Sand and ordinary earth favor him; rocky ground slows him; bedrock is difficult; concrete is a major obstacle; steel stops the method outright. While underground he can use vibration cues to read nearby movement.

Senses

His senses are heightened generally and become more effective nocturnally. Darkness is an advantage, not a separate supernatural transformation.

Equipment & Combat

Gila Monster's black-and-orange suit is flexible protective equipment built around mobility and survival. His gloves integrate short artificial black fingernails: engineered weapons rather than biological claws. The system supports venom delivery and projectile use while preserving firm limits against heavily protected targets.

His combat style favors ambush, sudden repositioning, subterranean approach, disengagement, and selective re-entry rather than standing still to trade hits.
