Overview

Ja’Kori Benson, known as Commotion, is a fully human Chicago vigilante whose specialty is street fighting itself. A former professional boxer turned underground fighter, he is at his most dangerous when a fight becomes messy, crowded, improvised, and chaotic.

Core combat rule: Ja’Kori’s weapon is the room. His body, awareness, and environment work together as one fighting system.

Biography

Ja’Kori belongs to the extended Walker family and has been raised by his uncle Daren Walker since he was a toddler. Daren functions as a parental figure, though the exact Benson/Walker parentage remains open.

Ja’Kori entered professional boxing as a young adult. He had legitimate talent, but the sport’s rules constrained the way his body naturally wanted to fight. He eventually recognized that he was not failing at fighting; he was failing at boxing. He left the sport and entered Chicago’s illegal underground fight circuit, where grappling, broader movement, dirty fighting, and environmental improvisation could flourish.

His masked career begins through family trouble, not a generic desire to patrol for crime. When older Walkers attempt to keep him out of dangerous family affairs for his own protection, Ja’Kori secretly begins intervening while masked.

Personality

Ja’Kori is cocky, young, reckless, stubborn, street-smart, and intensely family-loyal. His intelligence is practical and situational rather than academic or universal. He reads people, exits, criminal behavior, and deteriorating situations quickly, but he can be wrong.

He respects agreed rules in legitimate competition. When violence becomes real, sporting etiquette stops mattering.

Abilities

Commotion has no superhuman powers. He is human, Non-Ascendant, and unenhanced.

  • Exceptional human agility and body control.
  • Experience-based situational and combat awareness; not a supernatural danger sense.
  • Professional boxing foundation.
  • Underground-fighting experience and dirty-fighting instincts.
  • Environmental improvisation using walls, doors, furniture, railings, vehicles, stairs, dropped weapons, and opponent positioning.
  • Strong fighter-level conditioning and practical street intelligence.

Combat Style

Commotion is a dirty, momentum-based, improvisational street fighter with a boxing foundation. His fights should flow: the position created by one exchange becomes the setup for the next.

Close quarters favor him. Hallways, apartments, stairwells, clubs, warehouses, alleys, and crowded rooms give him more tools. Open ground and clean firing lanes remove many of his advantages.

Equipment

Purple ballistic full-face mask

A deep-purple, tactical, removable ballistic mask built for concealment and protection without hiding his golden-brown dreadlocks. It can mitigate gunfire but cannot erase impact trauma, concussion, neck injury, or the danger of exposed body areas.

Gun jammers

Commotion carries multiple crude homemade devices that shut down all guns within a 15-foot radius of the activation point. The effect is deliberately non-selective: his own firearm stops working too. Exact mechanism, duration, inventor, and reuse rules remain open.

His wider equipment philosophy stays minimal: protective clothing, jammers, a situational firearm if needed, his own body, and the environment.
