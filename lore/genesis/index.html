OLDROOT / FOUNDATIONAL EVENT / ASCENDANTS

GENESIS

The Saint Dorsey catastrophe that transformed exactly seventy-seven humans into Ascendants and opened the modern superhuman age without becoming the origin of everything in OldRoot.

ASCENDANTS CREATED 77 Exactly seventy-seven · original Genesis event only
BEFORE THE EVENT

Saint Dorsey was already part of the Abyron story.

The Hampton Dynamics site on Saint Dorsey is a specialized deep-ocean division involved in sonar, advanced ocean mapping, marine exploration, HVA-01 research, Ax-126 fabrication testing, materials research, energy research, and advanced study of Abyron.

On Genesis Day, the site holds approximately 600–700 kilograms of unusually high-grade ceramic Ax-126. Much of that stockpile falls in approximately the 7.6–8.0 kg UMWR class under Hampton’s standardized testing system.

This is not Hampton’s entire Abyron supply. It matters because the Saint Dorsey stock is unusually high quality.

THE INITIAL CATASTROPHE

The exact trigger remains open.

The exact villain, weapon, and final attack mechanism responsible for Genesis have not been established. What is established is the physical environment produced at Saint Dorsey.

The catastrophe combines enormous kinetic impact, extreme temperature, sudden pressure disruption, huge electromagnetic discharge, oxygen exposure, destruction of advanced Hampton infrastructure, structural shock, and contamination from surrounding engineered materials.

Those conditions push high-grade ceramic Ax-126 beyond its normally exceptional stability.

THE THREE-SECOND DELAY

Genesis does not begin at the first instant.

The ceramic Ax-126 lattice initially survives while absorbing and accumulating an extraordinary amount of energy.

One second. Two seconds. Three seconds.

Then the lattice reaches catastrophic cascading failure. Hundreds of kilograms of Ax-126 begin changing state essentially together. The delay is a physical consequence of the material reaching its failure threshold, not an arbitrary pause.

GENESIS-STATE ABYRON

This is not normal Abyron gas.

Normal gaseous Ax-126 is dark orange and dangerously Abyronically radiative. Genesis produces something much more extreme.

The Saint Dorsey event vaporizes Ax-126, partially ionizes and excites the element, vaporizes metals and electronics, pulverizes infrastructure, aerosolizes composite material, and drives a massive electromagnetic and energetic contamination event.

The resulting Genesis-state Abyron is brilliant orange because the highly excited, partially ionized Ax-126 emits intense orange light. It is mixed with vaporized steel, copper, aluminum, electronics, composites, structural debris, and destroyed Hampton energy systems.

THE BLACK GENESIS FRONT

The wave is black. The gas is orange.

The iconic black Genesis wave and bright-orange Genesis-state Abyron are not contradictory.

The advancing phase boundary absorbs and distorts enough visible light to appear nearly black from a distance. Behind and within that leading front is brilliant orange energized vapor.

The visual language is therefore a black surface-hugging advancing edge with bright-orange Genesis vapor behind and within it.

WHY IT HUGS THE SURFACE

Genesis-state vapor remains unusually dense.

Abyron’s abnormal density behavior is already visible during the original sinking-bubble discovery. In the Genesis state, the vapor does not simply rise harmlessly into the atmosphere.

It moves aggressively along streets, floors, terrain, building interiors, vehicles, and the island surface. This dense behavior helps produce the defining surface-bound geometry of Genesis.

BIOLOGICAL CATALYST

Genesis does more than poison tissue.

Genesis-state Abyron combines gas-like spread, radiation-like penetration, and a biological-catalyst effect capable of forcing a living human system toward a radically altered stable configuration.

The term biological catalyst is preferred over a simple “mutagen.” The event can alter DNA, cellular systems, nerves, organs, bones, muscles, metabolism, sensory architecture, biological electrical activity, and entirely new physical systems or functions.

Ordinary dark-orange Abyron vapor does not normally create Ascendants. Genesis is a uniquely extreme state and environment.

THE 77

Genesis exposes far more people than it transforms.

Exactly seventy-seven humans successfully become true Ascendants. Genesis does not consciously choose them.

The successful transition requires a rare convergence of viable exposure range, biological survivability, compatible physiological response, and neurological and biological coherence during transformation. Too little exposure can produce no permanent change. Too much can destroy the subject. Many systems fail to stabilize.

Only seventy-seven successfully complete a self-sustaining Genesis transition.

WHAT AN ASCENDANT IS

Not merely an enhanced human.

Genesis did not simply produce stronger, faster, more durable humans. Ascendants can become walking physical phenomena.

Some possess potential ceilings high enough to eventually contend with god-tier, cosmic, supernatural, or extraterrestrial threats. Not every Ascendant reaches that level, and not every Ascendant has the same ceiling, but the original 77 can exist on scales ordinary enhancement cannot explain.

The term Ascendant belongs specifically to those seventy-seven people created by the original Genesis event.

DOMAIN FORMATION

Genesis grants a domain, not merely a trick.

Genesis uses information already present in a person: biology, neurology, personality, emotional state, immediate circumstances, survival priorities, ingrained behavior, physical activity during exposure, and long-standing neurological patterns.

Those factors influence the physical domain an Ascendant anchors to. The result should not be reduced to superficial job-themed powers.

Valid domains can include plasma, force generation, spatial displacement, kinetic-event translation, vector control, gravitic attraction, electromagnetic interaction, matter preservation, and similarly broad physical phenomena.

FIRST POWER ≠ TRUE POWER

The first manifestation is only the first thing the brain figures out.

An Ascendant may initially believe the power is explosions and later discover the domain is matter excitation. Apparent super-strength can later reveal force generation. Teleportation can prove to be spatial adjacency. Electricity can expand into direct electromagnetic interaction.

The first manifested ability is not necessarily the full power, the true scientific nature of the domain, the ceiling, or the most dangerous application.

ASCENDANT POTENTIAL

The 77 are not equal.

Ascendants vary dramatically in ceiling, growth rate, power capacity, mastery, creativity, understanding, willingness to train, and physical tolerance.

Some remain relatively weak. Some are extremely powerful immediately. Some appear unimpressive for years before discovering terrifying potential. Some never understand more than a fraction of what they could have become, and some die before fully understanding their domain.

The moment supplies the need. The person supplies the interpretation. Genesis supplies the scale.

GENESIS DUST

The residue cannot create another true Ascendant.

Genesis Dust forms from material that never completes the full Genesis vapor transition: fractured and partially vaporized Ax-126, recondensed low-UMWR microcrystals, irradiated metals and minerals, altered structural material, and contaminated debris.

It retains a weaker echo of the Genesis catalyst. It can amplify physical output, senses, stamina, existing powers, energy production, or biological performance, but it cannot establish a new true Ascendant domain.

ARTIFICIAL GENESIS

Humanity can reproduce pieces of Genesis, not Genesis itself.

Governments and other organizations can gasify Abyron, expose subjects, manipulate concentration, add electricity or radiation, introduce metals, use drugs, alter genetics, control chambers, and adjust pressure and temperature.

Those programs can create supersoldiers, enhanced humans, unstable powered subjects, monsters, failed transformations, engineered anomalies, and catastrophic accidents. They cannot reliably reproduce the original Genesis event.

The original catastrophe involved hundreds of kilograms of unusually high-UMWR Ax-126, uncontrolled mass phase transition, ionization, metallic contamination, electromagnetic discharge, atmospheric conditions, Saint Dorsey infrastructure, enormous environmental complexity, and thousands of unique human biological states.

Later researchers can influence probabilities and outcome families, but Abyron empowerment does not become a guaranteed recipe for a specific power.

WHAT THE WORLD KNOWS

Abyron can be known without the full truth of Genesis being public.

Before Genesis, scientific, military, aerospace, and technological circles can know that Hampton discovered Element 126, while source locations, extraction details, real supply size, UMWR mechanics, bloom procedures, liquid reversion, gas hazards, military applications, and reservoir data remain restricted.

After Genesis, concealing Abyron’s involvement in the Saint Dorsey catastrophe becomes increasingly difficult. What Hampton and government interests have overwhelming reason to conceal is the full relationship between Genesis-state Ax-126 and the creation of the Ascendants.

If that relationship becomes widely understood, militaries, intelligence agencies, criminal organizations, corporations, rogue researchers, and governments all gain a reason to acquire Ax-126 and attempt another Genesis.

CANON BOUNDARY

Genesis is foundational, not universal.

Genesis is one major origin branch in OldRoot. It does not explain every hero, villain, magic system, artifact, alien, genetic anomaly, martial artist, cosmic phenomenon, experiment, or independent power source in the universe.

The exact Genesis villain, weapon, final attack plot, and the precise individual reason each member of the 77 survived remain intentionally unresolved.
