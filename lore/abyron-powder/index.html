ABYRON POWDER

A post-Genesis derivative of Ax-126 created by the Saint Dorsey catastrophe. Powder does not give a person something new—it forces more out of what is already there, temporarily and at severe biological cost.

CORE RULEAMPLIFIERExisting systems only · temporary overdrive · severe cost
CORE IDENTITY

Not crushed armor. Not portable Genesis.

Abyron Powder is a post-Genesis derivative of Abyron / Ax-126. Crushing an Ax-126 plate produces broken ceramic Abyron, not genuine Powder. True Powder requires the extreme Genesis process.

During Genesis, Ax-126 was subjected to catastrophic structural failure, extreme heat, enormous electromagnetic discharge, pressure shock, partial vaporization, partial ionization, Abyronic radiation, vaporized metals, destroyed electronics, carbon/composite materials, structural debris, recondensation, and pulverization. Some Ax-126 shattered, partially vaporized, recondensed, fragmented into microscopic crystals, and fused with destroyed Saint Dorsey material instead of completing the full bright-orange Genesis-state vapor transition.

COMPOSITION & VISUAL IDENTITY

Damaged Ax-126 carrying Genesis history.

Powder can contain degraded Ax-126, vapor-condensed Abyron microcrystals, fractured ceramic Abyron, microscopic metallic material, carbon/composite fragments, traces of electronics, structural debris, residual Genesis-state energetic alteration, and residual Abyronic radiation behavior. It remains chemically related to Abyron but no longer behaves like intact Abyron.

It is extremely fine, predominantly bright orange with darker blood-orange flecking, subtly ceramic/metallic under direct light, and capable of tiny spark-like reflections. The blood-orange material can still be pure Abyron in its secondary crystalline arrangement. Powder does not constantly glow. Close/heavy exposure may carry a faint metallic/mineral smell and can leave a dry metallic taste at the back of the throat.

MATERIAL BEHAVIOR

Genesis permanently broke the coherent lattice.

Genuine Powder cannot properly undergo volumetric bloom. Pressure, decompression, oxygen, cooling, and confinement do not rebuild it into premium ceramic Abyron. It also cannot be restored into pristine Ax-126, virgin liquid Abyron, high-UMWR ceramic, or original HVA-01 material.

Its UMWR behavior is fragmented and non-coherent. Enormous numbers of damaged microscopic structures can produce noisy and inconsistent Ax-like anomalous weight readings instead of one stable batch-wide ceiling.

FUNDAMENTAL BIOLOGICAL RULE

Abyron Powder does not give you something new—it forces more out of what is already there.

Powder is far more biologically reactive than stable ceramic Ax-126 because of its surface area, broken crystal edges, particle size, residual Genesis-state energetic alteration, Abyronic radiation, and contaminated microstructure.

It amplifies systems already present: biological systems, physical capabilities, altered physiology, superhuman abilities, and Ascendant domains. It does not normally create new powers, new Genesis domains, or true Ascendants.

HUMANS & POWERED USERS

Temporary overclocking follows the system already under stress.

A normal human remains fundamentally human. Possible temporary effects include increased strength, reaction speed, endurance, sensory processing, pain tolerance, cardiovascular performance, clotting, healing response, alertness, and physical output. Powder does not provide a clean universal stat increase; it tends to amplify whichever biological systems are being pushed hardest at that moment.

If someone already possesses a superhuman system, Powder drives that system harder. Super strength becomes temporarily stronger, regeneration accelerates, energy projection increases, heightened senses become more sensitive, and enhanced durability can rise. It does not add an unrelated ability.

TRUE ASCENDANTS

Genesis-born domains have unusually strong affinity.

True Genesis Ascendants have an unusually strong affinity for Abyron Powder and can react especially strongly to relatively small amounts. Possible effects include enormous temporary output, accidental domain expansion, loss of fine control, new expressions of an existing domain, and access to levels they have never consciously reached.

Powder may force an Ascendant into an ability expression they did not know they possessed. The expression was already latent inside the Genesis domain; Powder did not create it. The Ascendant may later learn to reproduce it naturally through training and understanding, but Powder does not permanently unlock mastery, teach control, or replace training.

Hard rule: Abyron Powder cannot create a true Ascendant.

EXPOSURE, ONSET & ACTIVE WINDOW

Direct tissue entry is the dangerous route.

Major routes include inhalation, injection, open wounds, and—less efficiently—ingestion. Injection is extremely efficient and dangerous. Intact-skin contact is substantially less dangerous than inhalation or injection, although contamination still requires serious handling.

Injected or heavily inhaled Powder can begin acting within seconds to a few minutes depending on purity, exposure, physiology, health, delivery method, and powered/Ascendant status. Significant enhancement commonly lasts roughly 10–30 minutes; this is an operational range, not a rigid timer.

THE CRASH

The body pays for the overclock.

Possible symptoms include shaking, vomiting, dehydration, extreme fatigue, muscle tearing, migraines, nosebleeds, arrhythmia, numbness, sensory disruption, memory gaps, pain, temporary weakness, and neurological instability. Powered individuals may temporarily lose control or experience suppression of their abilities afterward and can remain weaker than normal baseline for hours or days.

TOLERANCE, DEPENDENCY & LONG-TERM DAMAGE

Benefit decreases while required exposure and danger increase.

Repeated use produces tolerance while the damaging components do not become harmless. Chronic use can cause cardiovascular damage, nerve deterioration, organ stress, scarred lungs, cellular damage, abnormal tissue growth, altered pain response, hormonal disruption, chronic fatigue, neurological instability, shortened lifespan, and Abyronic contamination. Rare users may retain small physical changes, but these are not a reliable clean upgrade path; permanent consequences are primarily damage.

Powder can create both physiological and psychological dependency. Normal performance can feel pathetic after repeated overperformance; for powered users, the knowledge of what they can do while on Dust can become especially destructive. Permanent consequences are primarily damage, not convenient upgrades.

OVERDOSE & POWER RUNAWAY

Amplification can continue after deliberate control fails.

Severe overload can produce extreme overheating, seizures, internal bleeding, heart failure, uncontrolled muscle contractions, organ failure, tissue breakdown, neurological collapse, and death. Powered users can experience power runaway.

An Ascendant overdose can become a major containment emergency because neurological failure does not necessarily shut off an enormous underlying domain. Specific catastrophic incidents remain intentionally open.

INTERNAL MICRO-CRYSTALLIZATION

Repeated exposure can leave microscopic orange deposits.

Ax-derived crystalline inclusions may remain in lungs, blood vessels, scar tissue, organs, damaged muscle, and other contaminated tissue. They are microscopic deposits—not Abyron armor plates growing inside a person.

Deposits can cause chronic inflammation, nerve interference, vascular irritation, tissue damage, chronic pain, low-grade Abyronic activity, increased sensitivity to future Powder exposure, and continuing radiation-related problems. Accessible deposits may sometimes be removed surgically; microscopic or deeply embedded material may be impossible to remove safely.

ABYRON EXPOSURE PROTOCOL

Stabilization and decontamination—not an antidote.

Hampton eventually develops a formal Abyron Exposure Protocol. Treatment can include immediate decontamination, removal of contaminated clothing, respiratory flushing/support, oxygen support, cardiac stabilization, neurological monitoring, seizure control, temperature management, radiation mitigation, hydration, organ support, removal of accessible crystal deposits, and long-term neurological and organ monitoring.

There is no universal injection that safely cancels Powder. Outcome depends on amount, purity, exposure route, time before treatment, patient health, powered status, crystal formation, and affected organs. Some damage remains permanent.

QUALITY CLASSES

Four broad classes define the Powder ecosystem.

These are broad worldbuilding classes rather than universal packaging labels. Genesis-Origin Powder is authentic material created during the original Saint Dorsey event: rarest, strongest, most biologically reactive, and most unpredictable, with the most authentic Genesis-state energetic history. Refined Genesis Powder is genuine material cleaned of excess debris and larger contaminants, becoming more consistent and manageable while potentially losing some raw chaotic potency.

Synthetic Ax Powder is an artificial Powder-like material made from intact Abyron through combinations of crushing, irradiation, vaporization, recondensation, electromagnetic treatment, metallic contamination, and thermal cycling. It can amplify systems but is weaker, less Genesis-reactive, and biologically different from genuine Saint Dorsey material. Cut Dust is black-market material mixed with stimulants, fillers, conventional drugs, synthetic Ax particulate, diluted genuine Powder, contaminants, or unknown additives.

No universal milligram dosage is canon. Use broad exposure classes: trace / low, active / combat, and overload / overdose.

WEAPONIZATION, DETECTION & STORAGE

Controlled spaces matter more than open air.

Powder can be aerosolized, but open-air weaponization is unreliable because particles disperse, settle, blow away, and lose useful concentration. Enclosed rooms, ventilation systems, tunnels, bunkers, sealed vehicles, underground facilities, and close-range delivery systems are much more dangerous.

High-grade ceramic Ax-126 can shield Abyronic radiation, but Powder is also physical particulate. Effective protection requires Ax shielding plus sealed suits, respiratory filtration, and contamination controls; Abyron armor alone is not Powder immunity. Specialized equipment can identify Abyronic radiation, Ax-126 particulate, unusual spectral behavior, and characteristic anomalous response; ordinary security equipment does not automatically detect Powder.

Storage should be dry, sealed, shielded, away from extreme heat, and away from strong electromagnetic exposure. Strong energy exposure can increase Powder activity, and premium Powder containment may itself incorporate ceramic Abyron shielding.

FINITE SUPPLY, CRIME & MILITARY USE

Authentic Saint Dorsey Dust cannot be manufactured on demand.

Genesis-Origin Powder is finite. The largest known legitimate stores naturally belong to Hampton Dynamics and U.S. government, military, and research authorities, which controlled most cleanup, recovery, investigation, decontamination, and post-Genesis research. Exact stockpile quantities remain open.

Smaller amounts reach foreign militaries, foreign research programs, defense contractors, private laboratories, criminal markets, and smugglers through theft, diversion, corrupt transfer, cleanup mishandling, resale, smuggling, and military/research leakage. Genuine Genesis Powder still exists in meaningful quantities, but raw pristine Saint Dorsey material becomes increasingly rare; most later “Dust” is more likely refined genuine Powder, Synthetic Ax Powder, or Cut Dust. In criminal and scientific circles, “Real Saint Dorsey Dust” carries enormous weight.

Los Moralistas eventually participate in the criminal Abyron/Powder ecosystem, but their first shipment, supplier, first user, first operation, and first Powder-powered fighter remain open. Governments experiment with restricted emergency combat enhancement, powered-agent amplification, supersoldier research, special operations, medical research, and last-resort battlefield use. Foreign governments can be more cautious than the U.S., less cautious, or dramatically less ethical; named programs remain open. Illicit forms can include raw Powder, inhaler cartridges, injectable ampoules, pills/capsules, aerosol systems, and combat stimulants.

NAMING & HARD BOUNDARIES

Abyron Powder is formal. Dust is street language. Genesis Dust is a nickname.

Abyron Powder is the scientific/material term. Dust is strong street slang. Genesis Dust is a valid media/public nickname for the same underlying material, not a second substance.

Intact Abyron is humanity’s technological miracle. Genesis is humanity’s catastrophic transformation event. Abyron Powder is humanity trying to weaponize and consume the aftermath. Powder is not Genesis in portable form and cannot create a true Ascendant. It can contribute to later enhanced humans when combined with genetics, cybernetics, drugs, surgery, Abyron exposure, or other technologies; those subjects remain Non-Ascendants unless they belong to the original Genesis roster.

CREATIVE-FREEDOM RULE

The mechanics are locked; specific future stories remain open.

Leave open: the first criminal Powder user, first Powder crime, first Los Moralistas Powder story, first military program, first Ascendant Powder user, exact stockpile sizes, black-market prices, universal milligram doses, individual addiction stories, specific overdose events, exact regional slang, named foreign supersoldier programs, first public discovery of Powder abuse, and exact Hampton Powder research-project names.

Do not reintroduce Powder as a clean permanent super-serum, a free Ascendant-creation method, ordinary crushed orange ceramic, infinite material, a way to restore spent Abyron, a guaranteed power-granting drug, a universal fixed-percentage stat buff, a harmless performance enhancer, or a cure-all compound. Its defining identity is temporary amplification + severe cost.

SIMPLE AUDIENCE EXPLANATION

What Dust does in one sentence.

Genesis destroyed some of the Abyron and turned it into a special orange powder. The Powder cannot give people brand-new powers, but it can temporarily make their body or existing powers work much harder than normal. The stronger the boost, the more damage it can do to them.

Five-year-old version: It makes what you already have stronger for a little while, but it can hurt you.
